# Stock Shredder hero, round 3: concepts tested against the DEADWORK ethos

Ground rules for all three tests:
- The footage is the subject. Only Daniel's original footage is used: Lexus LY650 RED 6K/8K (metal shop, waterjet, sewing, paint booth, Miami), plus the filmed mouse click.
- The technology stays near invisible. It shows up only as hairlines, small mono labels and real metadata, never as an effect.
- Cuts and dissolves only, like an editor. No slicing, glitches or flying tiles.
- Every label is real: file names, takes, timecodes, the app's own RED IPP2 Natural look, and keywords generated by the app's own keyword service.
- Each plays once at the current hero's size and position, then rests.

## 01 The Archive
The wall is a real index of the shoot: 480 frames, each from one of the 690 RED takes. It drifts slowly upward, like a filmmaker scrolling through years of work. The frame then goes quiet, and six frames stay lit. Those six dissolve into six playing clips, labelled.
- **Made by a filmmaker?** Yes. It's a contact sheet, the oldest tool in the craft.
- **Celebrates the footage?** Yes. Hundreds of real frames, then six chosen ones at full size.
- **Explains why Stock Shredder exists?** Yes. The archive is too big to sort by hand, and the good moments are buried in it.
- **Timeless?** Yes. A contact sheet and a selection, with no effects.
- **Premium in three years?** Yes, because it depends on the work, not the motion.
- **Risk:** the wall could read as a busy collage. It needs very slow motion and a strong dim.

## 02 The Edit
One exceptional take, the 5:18 welding-bay take (A002_C044_0217SC), plays large. A single hairline under it stands for the whole take. A playhead moves along it, and at each strong moment the picture hard-cuts there and a mark stays on the line. Six marks end up on one take, and the frame then plays the six selects in turn, each with its real timecode.
- **Made by a filmmaker?** Yes. This is exactly how an editor works through a take.
- **Celebrates the footage?** Yes. One shot at full size, the spark waterfall as the hero moment.
- **Explains why Stock Shredder exists?** Yes. A five-minute take holds six usable clips, and finding them is the tedious part.
- **Timeless?** Yes. A timeline and a cut.
- **Premium in three years?** Yes.
- **Risk:** the timeline must not turn into fake software UI. It stays one hairline with marks.

## 03 The Transformation
The hand presses the mouse (the real filmed click). Then the camera-original file appears exactly as it comes off the card: RED Log3G10, flat and grey, with its real file name. Stock Shredder's own look (RED IPP2 — Natural) settles in through one slow dissolve. Its export line follows (ProRes 422 HQ · 4K), then its real keywords.
- **Made by a filmmaker?** Yes. Log to graded is the moment every DP knows.
- **Celebrates the footage?** Yes. The image gets more beautiful in front of you.
- **Explains why Stock Shredder exists?** Yes, most directly: one click takes a raw file to a stock-ready clip.
- **Timeless?** Yes. A single dissolve.
- **Premium in three years?** Yes, as long as the type stays small and quiet.
- **Risk:** the labels could feel like a spec sheet, so they need to stay very small and timed slowly.
