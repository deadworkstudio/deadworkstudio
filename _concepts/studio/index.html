Option 01

Editorial Minimalism

A fashion editorial on cream. Mostly empty space, an oversized headline split across the full width, and two photos placed like magazine plates.

Strongest

  • "CREATE MORE." flush left, "MANAGE LESS." flush right: confident and simple.
  • The values read like a lookbook: big 01–06 numerals, entries stepping across the grid.
  • The email address set huge at the close feels like a magazine back page.
  • Slow, soft reveals; very calm.

Weaknesses

  • Section labels (Values, Join, Contact) are very small and easy to miss.
  • Long and sparse; it can feel thin to someone skimming.
  • Least "industrial" of the five; furthest from the DEADWORK equipment feel.

Option 02

NASA / Industrial

A dark mission-control spec sheet. Bone type on a faint hairline grid, with ruler ticks, registration marks and crosshairs on the photos. No technical words added: the precision comes from structure alone.

Strongest

  • The six values as a control panel of six modules, each with a small indicator light that powers on in sequence.
  • The mission-control photo measured into the grid beside the headline.
  • Lines draw in and photos wipe open; crisp and mechanical.
  • The shortest page: dense, fast to scan.

Weaknesses

  • The headline is smaller than in the others; less of a statement.
  • Empty space under the headline on desktop.
  • The NASA lettering in the photo is someone else's mark; worth a thought before going live.

Option 03

Cinematic Manifesto

The page plays like a short film. The headline is a title card over your behind-the-scenes footage, the paragraphs sit over the LA skyline, and each value is a full-width frame with its own photo. Join and Contact close like end credits.

Strongest

  • The most emotional opening: real film footage behind the headline.
  • "To make things." lands as its own big line, a moment the other concepts don't create.
  • Every value gets its own image: engines, consoles, spacesuits, server racks.

Weaknesses

  • The longest page by far; six full-width image frames is a lot of scrolling.
  • Heaviest to load (video plus seven photos).
  • The images are the same ones the product menu uses, so they repeat across the site.
  • The email address was made a tap-to-email link (a small behavior change from today).

Option 04

Brutalist / Architectural

Dark with a poured-concrete grain. CREATE / MORE. / MANAGE / LESS. stack like building blocks, cropped at the screen edges, over a tall slab of server racks. Values, Join and Contact follow as heavy flush slabs.

Strongest

  • The most striking first screen; impossible to mistake for a template.
  • The values as a stepped stack of slabs in three finishes; value 03 (the AI line) gets the widest one.
  • "JOIN" and "CONTACT" set enormous; blunt and memorable.

Weaknesses

  • Busiest of the five; on phones the headline overlaps the photo.
  • Furthest from the calm Stock Shredder page; the two wouldn't feel like siblings.
  • Small layout rough edges ("Values" overlaps the first slab, kicker wraps on phones).
  • The email address was made a tap-to-email link.

Option 05

DEADWORK Evolved

Recommended

Built on the Stock Shredder page's own language (cream, then a black image block, then hairline-ruled content), with one huge headline and the Studio's LA skyline photo used full-screen as the home-page moment.

Strongest

  • Reads as Stock Shredder's sibling: same tone blocks, same section heads, same pill buttons.
  • The headline at full width rising out of masked lines on load: one meaningful motion moment.
  • The two paragraphs sit in the dark sky between the studio light and the city.
  • Values laid out like the Stock Shredder spec sheet; easy to scan.
  • Compact page, nothing wasted.

Weaknesses

  • The safest of the five; less of a surprise than 03 or 04.
  • Lots of empty cream under the headline on tablet.
  • On phones the second paragraph sits over the city lights (kept readable with a dark fade).
