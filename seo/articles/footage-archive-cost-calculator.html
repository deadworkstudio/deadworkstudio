    How this is worked out

      Paying to store footage you never use?

      Some of an archive is good stock footage. Stock Shredder finds the usable shots in raw footage, trims and grades them, and exports each one as its own clip with a title and keywords. Try it on 100 clips free.

      The three options, honestly.

      • Hard drives. Cheapest to start and easy to plug in. They wear out, so the real cost includes buying them again and copying everything across.
      • LTO tape. A big first cost for the drive, then cheap cartridges that hold a lot. The catch is generations: the LTO Program says LTO-10 drives don't read older tapes, so moving up means copying across.
      • Cloud. Nothing to buy and it's off site. You pay every month for as long as the footage is there, and the bill grows with the archive.
