    Where to find your numbers.

    Every contributor dashboard shows downloads and earnings. Use the last 12 months if you have them. One good or bad month can swing the result a lot.

    • Clips online. Count each clip once, even if it's on several sites.
    • Downloads a month. Add up downloads across sites over 12 months, then divide by 12.
    • Earnings per download. Your total earnings over the same 12 months divided by those downloads. That's what actually reaches you, after each site's share.

    Why per clip is the number to watch.

    Total earnings grow when you add clips or when your clips sell better. Earnings per clip tell you which one is happening. If that number holds steady as your library grows, every batch of new clips adds roughly the same amount each month.

    The slider lets you be careful. If your best clips are already online, set new clips lower than 100% and see if the archive is still worth the time.

    Getting the archive online.

    For most shooters the clips already exist. They're on drives, waiting to be trimmed, graded, exported, named and keyworded. At about 2 minutes a clip by hand, 1,000 clips is 33+ hours.

    Stock Shredder finds the usable shots, trims them, applies a look, exports them and writes a title, description and keywords for each clip. You review and upload.
