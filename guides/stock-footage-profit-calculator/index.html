    Where to find your numbers.

    Every contributor dashboard shows downloads and earnings. Use the last 12 months if you have them. One good or bad month can swing the result a lot.

    • Clips online. Count each clip once, even if it's on several sites.
    • Downloads. Add up downloads across sites over the last 12 months and pick A year, or enter one month and pick A month.
    • Earnings per download. Your total earnings over the same 12 months divided by those downloads. That's what actually reaches you, after each site's share.

    Why per clip is the number to watch.

    Total earnings grow when you add clips or when your clips sell better. Earnings per clip tell you which one is happening. If that number holds steady as your library grows, every batch of new clips adds roughly the same amount each month.

    The slider lets you be careful. If your best clips are already online, set new clips lower than 100% and see if the archive is still worth the time.

    Getting the archive online.

    For most shooters the clips already exist. They're on drives, waiting to be trimmed, graded, exported, named and keyworded. At about 2 minutes a clip by hand, 1,000 clips is 33+ hours.

    FAQ

    How much does one stock clip earn?

    It depends on the clip, the site and the season, so this calculator doesn't guess. Enter your own downloads and earnings and it works out what your clips earn on average.

    Where do I find my earnings per download?

    In your contributor dashboard. Take what you earned over the last 12 months and divide it by the number of downloads in the same period.

    What share do stock sites pay for video?

    Adobe Stock pays 35% for video. Pond5 pays 40% if exclusive and 30% if not. Shutterstock pays 15 to 40% depending on your level. Your earnings per download already include this.

    Do more clips mean more money?

    Usually more clips mean more chances to be found, but every clip sells differently. That's why the calculator lets you set how well new clips sell compared with your current ones.

    Does this calculator save my numbers?

    No. It runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
