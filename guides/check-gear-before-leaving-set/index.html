The Two-Minute Gear Check Before You Leave Set

The truck is loaded and everyone wants to go. Here's a short last look that catches what the wrap missed.

The two-minute check

  1. Count the cases. The number matches your list.
  2. Look at every case's sign-off. None are unchecked.
  3. Walk the set with a light, room by room.
  4. Check the five spots: sockets, tripods, readers, stands, people.
  5. Ask once, out loud: "Anyone holding gear?"

Why a last look.

A good wrap checks what's in each case. It can't check what never got to a case: the charger still in the wall, the plate still on the tripod in the corner, the battery someone set on a windowsill. The last look is for those. It takes a couple of minutes and it happens after the truck is loaded, when nobody wants to do it.

If your team doesn't have a wrap routine yet, start with our film production wrap checklist. The last look is its final step.

The check, step by step.

  1. Count cases. Count what's on the truck or in the car against your case list. A missing case is the biggest loss and the fastest to spot.
  2. Check the sign-offs. Every case should have been checked at wrap. If one wasn't, open it now, not at the next shoot.
  3. Walk every room you used. Include the bathroom, the holding area and wherever lunch was. Use a flashlight, even in daylight.
  4. Hit the five spots. See the list below.
  5. Ask out loud. One question to the whole team: is anyone holding gear? Lens caps and markers live in pockets.
  6. Leave the location as you found it. Then go.

The five spots to check.

  • Wall sockets. Battery chargers, laptop chargers, power strips and extension cables.
  • Tripods and stands. Quick release plates, baseplates, clamps and small monitors.
  • Card readers and laptops. The last card of the day, still in the slot.
  • Flat surfaces. Windowsills, tables, apple boxes and the tops of carts.
  • People. Lav transmitters on talent, and anything a team member pocketed.

A paper version.

Write the five spots on the back of your case list, or use the printable wrap checklist, which ends with this last look. For building the case lists themselves, the camera gear checklist maker prints each item with a box for packing and a box for wrap.

FAQ

What is a last look on set?

A final walk of the location after the gear is packed, to catch anything left behind before you drive away. It's usually done by one person with a light.

How is this different from the wrap checklist?

The wrap checklist makes sure each case is complete before it closes. The last look checks the location for anything that never made it to a case.

What gets forgotten most when leaving a location?

In our experience: chargers in wall sockets, quick release plates on tripods, the last card in a reader, and anything set down on a stand or windowsill during the day.

Can my phone remind me to check before I leave?

Yes, with Loadout on iPhone. You can turn on a reminder for when your phone leaves the location, so a case you haven't checked doesn't drive away unnoticed.
