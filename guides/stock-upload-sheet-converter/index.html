    The order that works.

    • Upload the clips first. Every site matches a row to a clip by its file name, so the files have to be there before the sheet.
    • Keep file names identical. Same spelling, same extension. A renamed file won't pick up its row.
    • Then add the sheet. Upload CSV on Adobe Stock, the CSV button on Shutterstock's Submit page, Apply CSV on Pond5.
    • Check the categories. The converter picks them from your keywords. A quick look before you submit is worth it.

    To check titles and keywords one clip at a time first, use the free title and keyword checker. For the files themselves, the clip spec checker shows what each site would refuse.

    FAQ

    What columns does my sheet need?

    A file name column and a keywords column. Title and description are optional but worth having. The converter also reads sheets saved for Adobe Stock, Shutterstock or Pond5, so you can turn one site's sheet into the others.

    Why are the commas gone from my Adobe Stock titles?

    Adobe's CSV page asks for titles with no commas and no more than 70 characters, so the converter takes them out.

    Does Shutterstock's CSV have a title column?

    No. Shutterstock uses one description, so the converter puts your description there, or your title if a clip has no description.

    Where do I upload the CSV?

    Upload the clips first. Then use Upload CSV in the Adobe Stock contributor portal, the CSV button on Shutterstock's Submit page, or Apply CSV on Pond5.

    Is my sheet sent anywhere?

    No. It's read and converted in your browser, and the files are saved straight to your computer.
