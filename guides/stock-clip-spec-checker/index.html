The four things that catch most clips.

  • Length. A clip straight off the card is usually minutes long. Trim to 10 to 20 seconds and it fits every site.
  • File size. 4K ProRes HQ gets big fast. A 60 second clip can pass 5 GB, which is over Adobe Stock's 3.9 GB and Pond5's 5 GB for 4K.
  • Frame rate. 48 fps works on three sites but not Adobe Stock. Phone footage often changes frame rate inside the file, so export it at a steady rate.
  • Audio. Camera audio rides along on most exports. Three of the four sites would rather it wasn't there.

FAQ

Does this checker upload my video?

No. It reads the settings stored in the file's header, right in your browser. The video never leaves your computer, so even a 5 GB clip is checked in a second.

Which files can it read?

MOV and MP4, which is what all four sites want. Camera files like R3D, BRAW and MXF need to be exported first; the checker will tell you which sites take them as they are.

What is the safest export for every stock site?

ProRes 422 or 422 HQ in a MOV, at the camera's own size and frame rate, 10 to 20 seconds long, under 3.9 GB, with the audio off.

Why does Adobe Stock flag my 48 fps clip?

Adobe Stock's list of frame rates stops at 23.98, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 50, 59.94 and 60. Pond5, Shutterstock and Storyblocks all list 48.

Can the checker spot logos, shake or ungraded log?

No. It reads the file's settings, not the picture. Those checks still need your eye, or a tool that looks at the footage.
