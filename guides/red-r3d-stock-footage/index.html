RED R3D Footage to Stock Clips, Fast

R3D is huge, flat and loved by buyers once it's graded. Here's how to get a full card of it ready for stock without a week in the edit.

The short version

  1. Grade IPP2 log to a light Rec 709.
  2. Cut 10 to 20 second clips, one shot each.
  3. Export ProRes 422 HQ. UHD or DCI 4K is safe everywhere.
  4. Keep 6K or 8K for Pond5, which takes up to 8K.
  5. Audio off, unless the sound is the point.

Stock Shredder reads R3D and does it in one pass. 1,000 graded 4K clips took 3 hours 1 minute in our test.

Beautiful footage, heavy files.

Komodo, V-Raptor and older RED cameras record R3D that buyers love. But every shot still needs a trim, a grade from IPP2 log, its own render and a name before it can sell.

At about 2 minutes a clip, a 500-clip shoot is 16+ hours at the desk, and 6K or 8K renders don't make it faster.

RED footage, stock site rules.

  • Grade it. Log3G10 is flat on purpose. Adobe Stock won't take uncorrected log, and Shutterstock prefers Rec 709.
  • Raw R3D, sometimes. Pond5 takes R3D zipped with a MOV, up to 6 GB. Storyblocks takes R3D at 4K. Everyone else wants MOV or MP4.
  • Size. Pond5 goes up to 8K. Adobe Stock and Shutterstock are safest at UHD or DCI 4K. Never upscale.
  • File size caps. Adobe Stock 3.9 GB, Pond5 5 GB at 4K to 6K and 6 GB at 8K, Shutterstock 10 GB.
  • Frame rates. 48 works on Pond5 and Shutterstock, not Adobe Stock.

Sources: Adobe Stock technical requirements, Shutterstock video requirements, Pond5 footage guidelines, Storyblocks video requirements. Checked November 2026.

The by-hand way.

  1. Import the R3D files into Resolve, Premiere or REDCINE-X Pro.
  2. Scrub and set an in and out for each shot.
  3. Grade IPP2 to Rec 709 and set the output size.
  4. Render each shot as its own ProRes file.
  5. Name the file and its folder.
  6. Write a title and keywords.

Our average is about 2 minutes per clip, every clip.

The Stock Shredder way.

Install RED's free REDCINE-X Pro, then point Stock Shredder at the card dump on your Mac or Windows computer.

  • It reads R3D. With REDCINE-X Pro on the same computer, R3D opens like any other file.
  • It finds the moments. Usable shots are found and trimmed inside the length you set.
  • It grades. A built-in RED Look, or your own LUT. Fine-tune exposure, contrast, temperature and tint.
  • It exports. ProRes 422 HQ up to 8K, graded, log or both in one pass.
  • It checks. Logos, HDR, wrong frame rates and footage under 2K are flagged before you upload.
  • It names and keywords. Clean names, a folder per clip, and keywords ready to paste.

What to know before you start.

Stock Shredder handles the repetitive parts. A few things stay with you.

  • Install REDCINE-X Pro first. It's free from RED, and then R3D just works.
  • You review. It finds the usable shots. Your eye picks the ones that sell.
  • You upload. Names and keywords are ready to paste into each site.
  • It keeps the log copy too. Export graded and log in one pass, for buyers who want to grade from scratch.

When by hand still makes sense.

  • You want to upload the raw R3D itself to Pond5 or Storyblocks.
  • Hero shots that need raw settings pushed shot by shot.

For a full card, every clip needs the same steps. That's the part worth handing off.

FAQ

Can I upload R3D files to stock sites?

A few sites take raw R3D. Pond5 accepts R3D zipped with a MOV, up to 6 GB, and Storyblocks accepts R3D at 4K. Adobe Stock and Shutterstock want MOV or MP4, so most shooters deliver ProRes.

What grade should RED footage have for stock?

A light IPP2 to Rec 709 conversion. Adobe Stock won't take uncorrected log and Shutterstock prefers Rec 709. Buyers grade it again.

Do I need REDCINE-X Pro to use R3D in Stock Shredder?

Yes. Stock Shredder reads R3D when RED's free REDCINE-X Pro is installed on the same computer.

Should I downscale 6K or 8K RED footage for stock?

Only if the site doesn't list your size. Pond5 takes up to 8K. For Adobe Stock and Shutterstock, UHD or DCI 4K is the safe export.

How long does it take to prepare 1,000 stock clips?

By hand, about 33 hours at 2 minutes a clip. In our test, Stock Shredder shredded and exported 1,000 graded 4K ProRes clips in 3 hours 1 minute, not counting review.
