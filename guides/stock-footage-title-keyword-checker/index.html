      What gets clips rejected or buried.

      • Brand names. Harley, iPhone, GoPro, Jeep. Use the plain word a buyer would type: motorcycle, smartphone, action camera, off-road vehicle. Brands generally belong only on clips marked editorial.
      • Camera details. 4K, 60fps, S-Log3, 24mm. The site already shows the specs. Shutterstock asks for none of it in the metadata.
      • The same word twice. "Motorcycle" and "motorcycles" fill two slots with one idea. Search matches both.
      • Filler. "Stock footage", "video", "royalty free". Every clip on the site is all of those.
      • A keyword list as a title. Sites want a sentence. Commas in a title are usually a sign it should be rewritten.

      How to write a title buyers find.

      Say what a buyer sees, in the order they'd say it: who or what, doing what, where, and when.

      BeforeAfter
      Harley motorcycle 4K, rider, mountain road, sunset, cinematicRider on a motorcycle on a winding mountain road at sunset
      DJI drone aerial city 60fpsAerial view of a city skyline at dusk with traffic below

      Then put the words from the title at the top of your keywords, and add the rest underneath: the setting, the mood, the season, the concept. The checker above does that reordering for you.

      FAQ

      How many keywords can I use on Adobe Stock?

      Up to 49. Adobe gives the first 10 the most weight, so put the words that describe the shot best at the top.

      How many keywords does Shutterstock need?

      At least 7 and at most 50. Shutterstock also asks for no camera information and no trademarks in commercial clips.

      How long should a stock footage title be?

      Under 70 characters works on every site. Adobe suggests under 70, Storyblocks takes 16 to 140 and suggests about 65, and Shutterstock allows up to 2,048.

      Can I put 4K or my camera name in the keywords?

      It's best not to. Buyers search for what's in the shot, the site already shows resolution and frame rate, and Shutterstock asks for no camera information.

      Can I use brand names in stock footage keywords?

      Not for commercial clips. Use a plain word instead, like motorcycle for Harley or smartphone for iPhone. Brands are only allowed on clips marked editorial.

      Does this checker save what I type?

      No. It runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
