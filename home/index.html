Pre-Production IN DEVELOPMENT

Scene Lock

Organizes pre-production with storyboards, shot lists, camera plans, references, and schedules. Provides iPhone capability so everyone can see the shots with a clock to see where production is.

PlatformMac & Windows
Works withiPhone companion app, PDF & image references

Pre-Production IN DEVELOPMENT

Set Control

Keeps film sets organized with tracked schedules, locations, shot progress, timing, and production tracking. A change everyone sees via iPhone notifications. Eliminates inefficient communication during pre-production and production.

PlatformiPhone, Mac & Windows
Works withiPhone companion app, Scene Lock

Production IN DEVELOPMENT

Loadout

Tracks production gear, cases, and equipment so nothing gets lost or left behind. Using a phone, video and photos are uploaded to check list items.

PlatformiPhone, Mac & Windows
Works withiPhone companion app

Production IN DEVELOPMENT

Handoff

Safely transfers, verifies, and organizes camera media and production files. Eliminates guess work and gives confidence to the team or user.

PlatformiPhone, Mac & Windows
Works withSD, CFexpress & CompactFlash cards, external drives

Post IN DEVELOPMENT

Sound Driver

Real artists. Real sounds.

Auto sound design analyzes visuals of an edit, sourcing real sounds from artists, taking care of the grunt work to get to the creative process.

PlatformMac & Windows
Works withPremiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, Avid Media Composer, Pro Tools, Nuendo
