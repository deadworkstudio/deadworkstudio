Scene Lock Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Scene Lock is a scouting and shot-planning app for iPhone, Android and Mac. This page covers what it uses and why. Your scouts and plans are yours. We don't sell them and we don't track you.

What Scene Lock uses, and why

Camera. Used only to take scout photos and video and to show the viewfinder.

Location (GPS). Used only to tag scout photos with where they were taken and to draw the sun path. Your location leaves your phone only with photos you choose to send. The photo files themselves have their location data removed.

Motion and compass. Used on your device only, to point the viewfinder and sun path. This never leaves your device.

What we store, and for how long

When you share a day or a project, it goes through our server. We keep it only as long as it's needed:

  • Shared days: kept for 7 days per share code, then deleted.
  • Scout photos you send: kept until your Mac picks them up, then deleted.
  • Send to Set Control packages: kept until they're imported, and never longer than 90 days.

Backups stay on your Mac or in a folder you choose. They are never sent to us.

What we never do

  • No ads.
  • No tracking.
  • We never sell your data.
  • No third-party analytics in the app.

For everything else, including payments, see the DEADWORK Privacy Policy.
