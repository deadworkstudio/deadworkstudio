Handoff Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Handoff copies and verifies your footage on a Mac or Windows computer. The Handoff Remote app on iPhone lets you watch those copies from your phone. This page covers what each one uses and why. Your footage never leaves your computer. We don't sell data and we don't track you.

Your footage

Handoff reads your cards and writes your copies on your own computer and drives. Your footage, file names and checksums are not sent to us.

The iPhone app

Camera. Used only to scan the pairing code shown on your computer. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Local network. Used to find your computer when you are on the same network.

Notifications. If you turn them on, we use your phone's notification address to tell you when a card is safe or has failed.

What passes through our server. To show progress on your phone, your computer sends the card name, percent done, files and amount copied, time left, and the verify result for each drive. We also keep a pairing ID and your phone's notification address. We keep these only while your phone is paired. Unpairing removes them.

Your account and subscription

To start a free trial or subscribe, we use your email address, a code that identifies your computer (not your name), and your subscription status. Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see or store your card number. We use your email only to send sign-in codes and receipts for Handoff.

What we never do

  • No ads.
  • No tracking.
  • We never sell your data.
  • No third-party analytics in the iPhone app.

For everything else, see the DEADWORK Privacy Policy.
