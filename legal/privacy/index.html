Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

This covers every DEADWORK app — Scene Lock, Stock Shredder, Handoff, Loadout, Sound Driver, and Set Control. The short version: your footage and production data are yours. We don't sell it, we don't train on it without asking, and we don't hold it hostage behind a subscription.

What our apps collect, and why

Your project files. Footage, gear lists, production data, and anything else you bring into a DEADWORK app is processed to do what you asked the app to do — organize it, grade it, export it, track it. Wherever possible, that processing happens on your own device.

Your own cloud account. Where an app syncs data so it follows you across devices (like Loadout's gear lists and kits), it syncs through your own private iCloud account, not a DEADWORK server. That data stays under your control, the same way Apple's own apps (Photos, Notes) work.

Account and billing data. For apps with a DEADWORK-managed subscription (outside the App Store), we store the minimum needed to run your account — your email, plan, and usage against your plan's limits. We never see or store your card details directly; that's handled by our payment processor.

Protecting against piracy. Stock Shredder checks in with our server about once a day to confirm your plan, using an ID for your computer. Each exported file carries a short owner tag — a scrambled code, never your name or email — so stolen or cracked copies can be traced. If an activation key is entered incorrectly many times, or a key or account is used on a second computer, we record that event (including the IP address for repeated wrong keys) so we can stop abuse.

Optional features. A few apps offer optional features that use more of your device's data — for example, location, to notice when you've left a shoot. Anything like this is off by default, explained at the point you turn it on, and never sends more than that one feature needs.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data to anyone.
  • We never use your footage or production data to train AI models without asking you first.
  • We don't run ad tracking or third-party analytics SDKs that profile you.
  • We never lock you out of data you already created if you stop subscribing — your existing projects, files, and settings stay on your device and in your own cloud account either way.

Subscriptions and payment

Most DEADWORK apps offer a free trial, after which continued use requires a paid subscription (monthly or yearly). Depending on the app, that's billed through the App Store or directly by DEADWORK — in either case, DEADWORK never sees or stores your full payment details. See our Terms of Use for the full subscription terms.
