# Round 2 / B: Editorial Scale

## What this is
Cinematic Studio's mood, but the product names themselves are treated as headline-scale typography — a dedicated "roster" section (`<section class="roster">`) up top where all 6 names run large enough to compete with "CREATE MORE. MANAGE LESS." Below that, the same 3-chapter structure as the original concept.

## What stayed from Cinematic Studio
- Widescreen black-and-white stills, same 3-chapter scroll (I/II/III), same restrained color use.
- Same live clock, reused `notify.js` signup forms, same reduced-motion handling for the hero video.

## What's different
- An explicit "opening" + "roster" pairing right after the hero: the headline, then the 6 product names at near-headline scale, so the product list isn't something you have to scroll to discover — it's part of the first impression.
- Typography does more of the storytelling work; the chapters below read more like confirmation/detail than discovery.

## Tradeoffs
- Running 6 product names at that scale takes real vertical space — this is a taller page than the original Cinematic Studio before you even reach the first chapter.
- Legibility at that size needed care (tight leading, careful line breaks on mobile) — worth a close look on a small phone screen.
