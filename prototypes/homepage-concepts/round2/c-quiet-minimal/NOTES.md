# Round 2 / C: Quiet Minimal

## What this is
The lightest-weight version of Cinematic Studio. Instead of 3 chapters with full-width crew-photo stills, it's two sections: a single opening "frame" (headline + hero video/poster) and one "bill" section — a film-poster-style credits block listing all 6 products in one place, grouped by category.

## What's cut
- The 3-chapter scroll and the ~9 extra black-and-white still images from the original concept are gone — no new image assets added, it leans entirely on the existing hero video/poster.
- Much shorter page — close to "one confident screen" rather than a multi-chapter scroll.

## What stayed
- Same widescreen cinematic typography and title-card feel for the hero.
- Same black-and-white restraint, same live clock (time only), same `notify.js` signup forms, same reduced-motion handling.

## Tradeoffs
- Noticeably less page weight and no extra image downloads — fastest of the four round-2 variants.
- Each product gets less visual spotlight than in the chaptered versions (no dedicated full-width still per category) — trades drama for speed and brevity.
