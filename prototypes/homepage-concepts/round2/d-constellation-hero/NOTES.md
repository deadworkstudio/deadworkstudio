# Round 2 / D: Constellation Hero

## What this is
The 3-left / logo-center / 3-right product constellation becomes the opening hero itself, not a section you scroll to. The DEADWORK wordmark sits dead center (`constellation-core`), "CREATE MORE. MANAGE LESS." is folded directly into that same opening block, and the 6 product names run as two columns flanking it — all in one first screen.

## How it reconciles with the headline
The headline doesn't get smaller or move later — it sits inside the constellation block itself, wrapped around the wordmark, so the first thing you see is headline + full product map together, instead of headline first and products discovered later by scrolling.

## What stayed from Cinematic Studio
- Same 3 chapters below the fold (I/II/III, each with a full-width still) for anyone who keeps scrolling — these act as the "detail" pass after the constellation gives the overview.
- Same black-and-white restraint, same live clock, same reduced-motion handling, same `notify.js` forms.

## Tradeoffs
- This is the busiest first screen of the four — headline, wordmark, and 6 product names are all visible at once, so it carries more information density than the original Cinematic Studio's quieter opening.
- Because the constellation already shows all 6 products up front, the chapters below risk feeling repetitive — they're now reinforcing what's already been seen rather than revealing something new.
