Scene Lock

Plan smarter. Execute better.

Organizes your pre-production with storyboards, shot lists, camera plots, references, and schedules all in one place. Provides iPhone capability so everyone can see the entire day with a clock and schedule to see where production is.

Platform
iPhone, Mac & Windows
Works with
iPhone companion app, PDF & image references
02 / 06I — Pre-ProductionIN DEVELOPMENT

Set Control

Every crew. Every detail. In sync.

Organizes film sets with tracked schedules, locations, shot progress, timing and production in one shared view. A change made once is a change everyone sees via iPhone notifications. Eliminates inefficient communication during pre-production and production.

Platform
iPhone, Mac & Windows
Works with
iPhone companion app, Scene Lock
03 / 06II — ProductionIN DEVELOPMENT

Loadout

Never leave gear behind.

Tracks production gear, cases, and equipment against a created list. It uses uploaded photos to confirm each item before it leaves or is needed so nothing gets lost or left behind.

Platform
iPhone, Mac & Windows
Works with
iPhone companion app
04 / 06II — ProductionIN DEVELOPMENT

Handoff

Every file. Verified.

Handoff copies to every destination in parallel and checks each file against the original before it will ever call a card safe. If anything couldn't be verified, it says so — the card stays marked unsafe until it's actually confirmed.

Platform
iPhone, Mac & Windows
Works with
SD, CFexpress & CompactFlash cards, external drives

Sound Driver

Real artists. Real sounds.

Eliminates the grunt work and watches visuals of an edit to get to the creative process. Every sound comes from DEADWORK's own exclusive library; you won't find it in any other stock library or sound tool. Recorded and designed in-house and available nowhere else, with thousands of diverse cinematic sound variations straight to you.

Platform
Mac & Windows
Works with
Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, Avid Media Composer, Pro Tools, Nuendo

Stock Shredder

Raw footage into stock-ready clips.

Stock Shredder helps find and prepare usable moments from your raw footage. You review what stays, fine-tune the look, and export individual clips ready for stock submission.

Mac & Windows
