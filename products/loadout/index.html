The scan

Pan the case. Watch it tick.

Pan across the open case. Each item the camera spots is marked Seen.
Anything it hasn't seen stays on the missing list.

Seen vs Confirmed

Seen
The camera matched it to your kit. A match is not a check.
Confirmed
You tapped it, or tapped All packed. Only confirmed items count as packed.

Look-alikes, items hidden under other gear and dark cases need your tap. A guess or a false match never counts as packed.
