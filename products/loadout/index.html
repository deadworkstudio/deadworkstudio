The problem

Gear gets packed in a hurry or used by new crew. A missing item on set can risk an entire production.

The solution

Loadout tracks production gear, cases, and equipment against your list. The phone's camera spots each item, you confirm it, and anything missing stays flagged before the kit leaves.
