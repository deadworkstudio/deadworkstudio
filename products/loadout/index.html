The problem

Gear gets packed in a hurry or used by new crew. A missing item on set can risk an entire production.

The solution

Loadout tracks production gear, cases, and equipment against your list. Each item is confirmed by the phone's camera before it leaves, so nothing gets lost or left behind.

The scan

One photo. Every item.

Photograph a packed case and everything Loadout sees checks off.
Anything it misses, tap to check by hand.
