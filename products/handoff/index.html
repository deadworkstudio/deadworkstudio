The result

Four colours. Each one tells you what to do.

When a copy ends, the whole window fills with one colour and a plain sentence. Every colour comes with a symbol and a word, so it reads the same on a bright set or a dark one.

  • Green Safe.

    Every clip is verified on 2 separate hard drives. You can format the card.

  • Amber Not safe yet.

    The copy is good, but one copy short. Add one more and it turns green.

  • Red Don't format.

    It failed. It names the hard drive and the clips. Re-copy just those.

  • Grey Interrupted.

    Power cut or a cable pulled. Resume, and it picks up where it stopped.

The copy rule

2 copies before safe.

Green means every clip is verified on 2 separate hard drives. Your production can ask for anywhere from 1 to 5. One copy short and the card ends amber: not safe yet, never green.

Backing up or transferring from a hard drive? The original can count as one copy, but only when Handoff confirms it is a hard drive. A card never counts. Green then says Original + 1 copy — keep the original, and it never tells you to format.

The record

Every card, on file. For good.

History is a permanent record of the production: every card, every hard drive, every result.
Each card leaves a PDF custody report and an MHL checksum file on every hard drive, for post houses and insurers. The report only says "safe" when the card was green.

The crew

Phones buzz green. Or red.

Walk away from the cart. The free iPhone app shows every card as it copies and buzzes the moment it turns green or red.
Add as many phones as you like. Phones only watch: nobody can change a copy from one.

  • iPhone, free. Live progress in the app, a live card on the lock screen while a card copies, and an alert the moment it turns green or red.
