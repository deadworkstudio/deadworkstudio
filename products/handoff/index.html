The workflow

Card in. Proof out.

  1. 01ConnectPlug in the card or drive. Handoff reads every file before touching anything.
  2. 02CopyCopies to every backup destination at once, not one after another.
  3. 03VerifyEvery copy is checksummed against the original. Not just a file-size match.
  4. 04ConfirmA clear verdict — SAFE TO WIPE, or exactly why not — then eject.

The problem

Camera cards get copied by hand, under a deadline, after a long day. One bad cable, one interrupted transfer, one card formatted a step too early — and a day's footage is gone with no warning it was ever at risk.

The solution

Handoff copies to every destination in parallel and checks each file against the original before it will ever call a card safe. If anything couldn't be verified, it says so — the card stays marked unsafe until it's actually confirmed.

The features

Built so you never have to guess

01

Copy, then prove it

Every destination is written in parallel and checksummed against the source — a match on file size alone was never good enough.

02

A verdict, not a guess

SAFE TO WIPE only appears once every file on the card has been read and verified. One unreadable file and the card stays flagged.

03

Eject the right thing

Ejects the card itself, checks it isn't still being read, and refuses to touch a drive that's still in use or isn't actually removable.

04

Watch it from your phone

Keep an eye on a transfer from across set with the Handoff companion app, without standing over the laptop.
