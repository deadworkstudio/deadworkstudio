The problem

Camera cards get copied by hand, under a deadline, after a long day. Second-guessing files with low confidence or one bad transfer, a day's footage is gone.

The solution

Safely transfers, verifies and organizes camera media automatically. Confirming every file copied clean eliminating guess work, giving confidence to the team or user.

01 · The verdict

Safe or failed. Nothing in between.

When every copy matches, the screen turns green. If a single file doesn't, it turns red, names the drive, and re-copies to just that drive in one click.

02 · Hands off

Plug in a card. It starts.

Handoff remembers your drives and project from the last card and starts copying on its own. If anything needs a look first, it stops and asks.

03 · The record

Every card, on file.

Every card, every drive, every result, in one history. Each safe card leaves a checksum report on every drive for post and insurance.

On set

  • 01

    Every drive at once

    A shuttle, a RAID and a backup, copied in one pass.

  • 02

    Read back

    Every file is read back from each drive and compared to the card.

  • 03

    Checksum reports

    An ASC MHL report on every drive, the record post houses and insurers ask for.

  • 04

    Re-copy one drive

    If one drive fails, re-copy to that drive alone. The others stay as they are.

  • 05

    Proxies

    720p proxies after every safe card, with audio and timecode kept.

  • 06

    Never overwrites

    Every card gets its own folder. A second card with the same name gets (2), never a replacement.

  • 07

    Folders your way

    Project, day and card. Project and card. Or card only.

  • 08

    Custody report

    A report for every card, for producers and insurance.

  • 09

    Card labels

    Print a label for the card right from the result screen.
