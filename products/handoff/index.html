Why Handoff

Every card accounted for. Every backup verified. Every person who needs to know, knows.

Cards get copied at the end of a long day, under a deadline. One bad copy and the footage is gone. Handoff does the copying, proves every file landed, and tells the people waiting on it.

01 · No setup

Plug in. Click once. Walk away.

There is nothing to set up first. Handoff finds your cards and hard drives by itself, and one click copies to the same drives as last time.

04 · The result

Four colours. Each one tells you what to do.

When a copy ends, the whole window fills with one colour and a plain sentence. Every colour comes with a symbol and a word, so it reads the same on a bright set or a dark one.

  • Green Safe.

    Every clip is verified on 2 separate hard drives. You can format the card.

  • Amber Not safe yet.

    The copy is good, but there aren't enough copies yet. Add one more and it turns green.

  • Red Don't format.

    It names the hard drive and the clips that failed. Re-copy just those.

  • Grey Interrupted.

    Power cut or a cable pulled. Resume, and it picks up where it stopped.

05 · The copy rule

2 copies
before safe.

Green means every clip is verified on 2 separate hard drives. Your production can ask for anywhere from 1 to 5. Copy to fewer than that and the card ends amber: not safe yet, never green.

  • Shuttle A + RAID Master2 of 2 copies · every clip matchedSafe
  • Shuttle A only1 of 2 copies · add one moreNot safe yet
  • Your rule1 to 5 copies · set once per productionSetting
