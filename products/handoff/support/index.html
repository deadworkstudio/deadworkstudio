Copying a card

There is nothing to set up first.

  1. Plug in the camera card and your backup hard drives.
  2. Handoff finds them by itself and opens on the card: what is on it, and which hard drives it will copy to. It picks the same hard drives as last time.
  3. Click once to copy. To use different hard drives, choose Change first.
  4. Wait for the colour. While it copies, the whole window is the progress bar: a big number, and real frames of the footage that is already checked. Every file is read back from each hard drive and checked against the card with xxHash64. When it ends, the whole window turns one colour.

There are three jobs. Copy takes a card to your hard drives. Backup copies one hard drive to another. Transfer sends footage from a hard drive to an editor or post house.

You don't need the internet to copy or check. Phones need a connection to get alerts.

What the colours mean

  • Green

    Safe. Every clip is verified on 2 separate hard drives. You can format the card. For a backup or transfer from a hard drive, green may say Original + 1 copy. Keep the original. instead (see below). That one doesn’t mean format.

  • Amber

    Not safe yet. Don't format. The copy is good, but it is one copy short. Usually only one hard drive was used. Choose Add a second copy and it turns green once the second copy is verified.

  • Red

    Failed. Don't format. Something didn't match. The window says which hard drive and which clips. Plug the hard drive back in if it came loose, then re-copy just those clips. The clips that matched stay as they are. If the same hard drive fails again, use another one.

  • Grey

    Interrupted. Don't format. The copy stopped before the end, for example the Mac lost power. Open Handoff with the card and hard drives plugged in and choose Resume. It keeps what was already verified and carries on.

Only green means the card is safe to format. Every colour also shows a symbol and a word, so you never have to judge by colour alone.

The 2-copy rule

One copy is not a backup. A hard drive can fail, get dropped or go missing, so Handoff only turns green when every clip is verified on 2 separate hard drives.

  • Copy to one hard drive and the card ends amber, "not safe yet". It never turns green with fewer copies than your rule.
  • You can change the number of copies from 1 to 5 in Settings (the gear, top right). Set it once per production.
  • Pick hard drives that are really separate. Two folders on the same hard drive count as one copy.

Backing up a hard drive: original + 1 copy

For a Backup or Transfer from a hard drive, the original can count as one of the copies, but only when Handoff confirms it is a hard drive. A card never counts as a copy. With the original plus one verified copy, the window turns green and says Original + 1 copy. Keep the original. It won’t tell you to format it. Keep the original until your production has the copies it needs.

Pairing a phone

  • On your Mac, open Handoff, go to the sharing screen and choose Add a phone. A code appears.
  • On your iPhone, open the free Handoff app and scan the code. You can also type the code instead.
  • The code works for 5 minutes. If it runs out, make a new one.
  • Add as many phones as you like. Every phone is free, and phones can watch but never change a copy.
  • The iPhone app will be free on the App Store when Handoff launches. It is waiting on Apple's review now.
  • Android is coming, with alerts.

No notifications

  • On the iPhone, open Settings, then Notifications, then Handoff, and turn on Allow Notifications.
  • Check the phone has a connection. The Mac copies offline, but alerts need the internet to reach your phone.
  • Still nothing? Remove the phone in the sharing screen on the Mac, then pair it again.

When it works, your phone buzzes the moment a card turns green or red, and shows a live card on the lock screen while a card copies.

Each person picks what their phone is told: safe, failed, not safe yet, interrupted, and the lock-screen card. Change it in the iPhone app's settings, or on the Mac in the sharing screen.

Share links

A share link is a read-only web page for a producer, client or insurer. It shows the card's result, clip count and each hard drive's check, and nothing else: no footage and no file names.

  • Make one from a finished card with its share button, or from History.
  • Choose how long it works: 1, 7 or 30 days.
  • To stop a link early, go to the sharing screen, find it under Share links and choose Revoke. It stops opening at once.
  • The page is a record of what was checked at that time. It doesn't update live.

Moving to a new computer

One subscription will cover one computer. To move it:

  1. Open Handoff on the new computer and choose I already subscribed.
  2. Enter your email. We email you a 6-digit code.
  3. Enter the code, then choose Sign out and use here.

Your old computer is signed out, even if it is lost or broken. Your phones stay free; pair them with the new computer from the sharing screen.

Subscription

  • At launch: $15 a month or $129 a year, with a 14-day free trial and no card needed. It isn't on sale yet.
  • One computer per subscription, with unlimited phones.
  • Manage or cancel from the Handoff license card in Settings.
  • If your subscription ends, you can't start new cards. Copies in progress, re-copy, History, reports and your footage keep working. Your footage and History stay yours.
