Support

Handoff Support

Email deadworkstudio@gmail.com and we will reply within 2 business days. Tell us your computer (Mac or Windows), your Handoff version, and what happened.

Pairing the iPhone app

  • On your computer, open Handoff, go to Phones and choose Pair a phone. A code appears.
  • On your iPhone, open Handoff Remote and scan the code. You can also type the code instead.
  • The code works for 5 minutes. If it expires, make a new one.
  • No computer yet? Tap See a demo in the iPhone app.

No notifications

Turn notifications on for Handoff Remote in iPhone Settings, then unpair and pair again.

Subscription

One subscription covers one computer, and any number of phones can watch it for free.

Moving to a new computer. Open Handoff on the new computer, choose I already subscribed, and enter your email. We send you a 6-digit code. Enter it, then choose Sign out and use here. Your old computer is signed out, even if it is lost or broken.

Manage or cancel from the Handoff license card in Handoff settings. Your footage and history stay yours if you cancel.
