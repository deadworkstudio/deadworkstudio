IN DEVELOPMENT

Keeps film sets organized with tracked schedules, locations, shot progress, timing, and production tracking. A change everyone sees via iPhone notifications. Eliminates inefficient communication during pre-production and production.

The problem

Things change, documents get outdated, a schedule shifts, a location gets moved, and times adjust. None of it reaches the people who need it until someone asks out loud.

The solution

Set Control tracks schedules, shot progress, and timing in one shared view, so a change made once is a change everyone sees.
