The problem

Things are constantly changing; documents get outdated, schedules shift, a location gets moved, and times adjust. None of it reaches the people who need it until someone asks out loud.

The solution

Organizes film sets with tracked schedules, locations, shot progress, timing and production in one shared view. A change made once is a change everyone sees via iPhone notifications. Eliminates inefficient communication during pre-production and production.

01 · Run the day

Fifteen minutes behind. Everyone knows.

Start each scene as it rolls. Set Control keeps the count, ahead or behind, and every phone follows.

02 · The details

The small things, handled.

A shared checklist with who owns each task, and food orders tallied and ready to send to the caterer.
