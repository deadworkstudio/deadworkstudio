The problem

Shots, lists, boards, plots and schedules are shared inefficiently. By the time everyone's getting on the same page, half the shoot day is gone.

The solution

Organizes your pre-production with storyboards, shot lists, camera plots, references, and schedules all in one place. Provides iPhone capability so everyone can see the entire day with a clock and schedule to see where production is.

01 · The plot

Camera. Light. Actor. Wall.

Draw the plot right on the shot. Click to place, drag to move, turn it with one key.

02 · The plan

Every day, built.

Drop scenes into shoot days. Call time, page count, running time and wrap add up as you go.

03 · The shoot

Know exactly where the day stands.

A live clock, the shot that's up and how far ahead or behind you are, with the wrap time it means.
