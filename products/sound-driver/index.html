Sound Driver

IN DEVELOPMENT

Real artists. Real sounds.

Auto sound design analyzes visuals of an edit, sourcing real sounds from artists. Eliminating the grunt work to get to the creative process with tens of thousands of diverse cinematic sound variations straight to you.

The problem

Sound design starts with hours of scrubbing through an edit looking for what needs a sound, then hours more hunting for the right one.

What it does

Sound Driver watches the visuals in an edit and surfaces real sounds from real artists that fit, cutting the search down to a review instead of a hunt.
