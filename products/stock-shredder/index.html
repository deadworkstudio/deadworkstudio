The problem

Hours of unused footage sit in the archives because the repetitive workflow of reviewing, trimming, grading, exporting, and tagging by hand causes constant derailment from other tasks.

The solution

Stock Shredder helps find and prepare usable moments from your raw footage. You review what stays, fine-tune the look, and export individual clips ready for stock submission.

Fully supported: MP4, MOV, MXF, MKV, AVI, M4V, MTS, M2TS, MPG, MPEG, WebM, 3GP, WMV. Covers standard DJI, Sony, and ARRI camera files.

Blackmagic RAW (.braw) is supported natively. RED R3D is accepted with REDCINE-X Pro installed. CinemaDNG sequences (DJI, Blackmagic) are supported on macOS. ARRIRAW/ARRICORE (.ari/.arx sequences and modern ALEXA 35-style .mxf) works with ARRI's free ART CMD tool installed.

Not yet supported: Canon Cinema RAW Light. Phantom Cine isn't read directly, but Windows users can convert it first with Phantom's own free PCC software. Nikon N-RAW also isn't supported.
