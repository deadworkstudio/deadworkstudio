Stock Shredder

BETA

Accelerate the selection, trimming, organization, export and keywords of stock footage for marketplaces. Allowing freedom of time to pursue other things without avoiding income.

The problem

Terabytes of footage never make it to sites, because sorting, trimming, coloring and tagging by hand costs more time than it's worth.

The solution

Reviews raw footage, selects and trims the usable shots, exports them, then writes the keywords ready for immediate upload. Approve clips remotely via phone.
