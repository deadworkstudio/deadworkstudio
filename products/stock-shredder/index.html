The problem

Hours of unused footage sit in the archives because the repetitive workflow of reviewing, trimming, grading, exporting, and tagging by hand causes constant derailment from other tasks.

The solution

Stock Shredder helps find and prepare usable moments from your raw footage. You review what stays, fine-tune the look, and export individual clips ready for stock submission.

Features

    Auto-naming

    Every shot gets a clear name and lands in its own folder.

    Stock-site checks

    Flags logos, HDR, wrong frame rates and footage under 2K before you upload.

  • 03

    Bad clip filter

    Broken, empty and too-shaky clips are set aside for you.

  • 04

    Auto stabilize

    Smooths shaky shots, and leaves a shot alone if smoothing would ruin it.

    Cleans visible grain at export, only in the shots that need it.
