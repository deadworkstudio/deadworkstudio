The problem

Terabytes of footage never make it to sites because sorting, trimming, coloring, exporting, and tagging by hand takes more time than it's worth. Stock footage is profitable but the repetitive workflow of hundreds of hours can cause constant derailment from other tasks.

The solution

Reviews raw footage, selects and trims the usable moments, exports, then writes keywords ready for immediate upload. Approve clips remotely via phone. Allowing freedom of time to pursue other tasks, turning unused footage into thousands of revenue-producing assets without avoiding income.

Not yet supported: Canon Cinema RAW Light. Phantom Cine isn't read directly, but Windows users can convert it first with Phantom's own free PCC software. Nikon N-RAW also isn't supported.
