By artists. For artists.

CREATE MORE. MANAGE LESS.

Most people who want to create more spend their time juggling software, managing files, organizing projects, and fighting through complicated workflows.

The problem is that most creative tools add more complexity instead of removing it — leading to wasted time, creative burnout, unfinished projects, and less energy for the work that actually matters.

We believe technology should serve creativity, not get in its way. Because the less time artists spend managing the process, the more time they have to create something worth remembering.

Values

  1. 01

    Kill the tedious work, not the people doing it.

    Software removes the repetitive production work so creatives can spend their time creating.

  2. 02

    Idea. Execution. Completion.

    The human owns all three. DEADWORK removes the friction between them.

  3. 03

    We don't want AI making the art for us.

    We want it doing the tasks that waste time.

  4. 04

    Honest about what's ready.

    We label what's shipping and what's still in progress — no polish over a product that isn't there yet.

Commitments

  • We pay artists for every sound. Sound Driver sources real sound design from real artists, and every artist gets paid for the sounds we use — not a flat licensing buyout that cuts them out later.

  • Your footage stays yours. Footage and production data you run through our tools is yours. We don't sell it, train on it without asking, or hold it hostage behind a subscription.

  • No fake numbers. We don't publish invented customer counts, made-up reviews, or screenshots of products that don't exist yet.
