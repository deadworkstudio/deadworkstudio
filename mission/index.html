By artists. For artists.

CREATE MORE. MANAGE LESS.

Most people who want to create more spend their time juggling software, managing files, organizing projects, and fighting through complicated workflows.

The problem is that most tools or projects add more complexity instead of removing it. Leading to wasted time, creative burnout, unfinished projects, and less energy for the work that we actually got into this to do. To make things.

We believe technology should serve creativity, not get in its way. Because the less time artists spend managing the process, the more time they have to create something worth remembering.
